AI can do the work for us. That doesn't mean we stop needing to learn. It just makes learning easy to skip.

Building things used to be hard. You ran into problems nobody had solved for you, and you learned your way through them. That struggle was where the learning happened.

Now most of it is done for us, and we sit and prompt. Roadmap uses the same AI to put the learning back: it plans the route, explains each step and checks your work, but you do the building.

How it works

From a goal to a finished project, one lesson at a time.

Start with any goal

"Build an ESP32 weather station." "Bake my first sourdough." "Learn Python with a todo CLI." Type it in your own words and choose how far you want to go: two weeks, a month, three months or six.

Already vibe coded a project? Point Roadmap at it and it teaches you how your own code works.

It starts where you are

A few quick questions set the starting point, so you don't sit through what you already know. Then you check the plan before anything is built.

Learn, then do

  • Hand-picked videos, clear explanations and charts
  • Real tasks, quizzes and practice exercises
  • A milestone project to prove each stage
  • A tutor that reads your lesson before it answers

See how far you've come

Set a daily goal, keep your streak and watch the year fill in. Roadmap tracks the hours you spend learning, per day and per roadmap.
